DATOLENS — EARLY BETA / BETA TEMPRANA
0.1.0 · Apple Silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 12 or later

ENGLISH

Drag Datolens.app to Applications. The app explores CSV, XLSX, Parquet and
SPSS SAV files on your Mac. Your original files are preserved. Views and
results are stored locally. Optional AI needs your own provider key and
may incur provider charges; review the selected fields and scope first.

This early beta is signed with a local development certificate, NOT an
Apple Developer ID certificate. It is NOT notarized by Apple and macOS
may block it. Prefer the future notarized or App Store version if you need
the standard Apple-verified installation. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
Apple's guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
