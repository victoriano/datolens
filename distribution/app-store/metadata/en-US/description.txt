Big files. Clear thinking.

Datolens helps you explore data files on your Mac. Open that fresh export, understand its variables and find an angle you can turn into a chart.

YOUR FILES, IN ONE WORKSPACE
Open CSV, Excel (.xlsx), Parquet and SPSS (.sav). Work with multiple tables and sheets. Inspect distributions, missing values and statistics to get your bearings before an analysis.

FOLLOW A CLUE
Combine filters, select ranges and compare groups. Explore a sample of a large dataset or work with every row when you need to dig deeper. Opening time depends on the format, size and your Mac; CSV and Excel require an initial import.

MAKE IT VISIBLE
Build bar charts, scatter plots, time series and pivot tables. Choose your axis variables and export charts as PNG or SVG and results as CSV.

MADE FOR CURIOUS PEOPLE
Data analysts, journalists, researchers and product and operations teams: start with the files you already have. You do not need to code to explore and create charts. SQL queries and formulas are available when you want to go further.

EXPLORE LOCALLY
Local file exploration runs on your Mac without an account. Views and results are saved locally. When opening remote sources, Datolens connects to their origin server.

OPTIONAL AI, WITH AN EXPLICIT SCOPE
AI features require your own API key, may incur provider charges and send the selected context for the operation. Opening a file does not turn AI on. Keys are stored in macOS Keychain.

For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 12 or later.
